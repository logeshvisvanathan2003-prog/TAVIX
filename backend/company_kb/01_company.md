# TAVIX company overview
## What is TAVIX
TAVIX is the brand of TavixAI Technologies Solution Private Limited. It is a technology start-up that builds software, AI and hardware-plus-software products that solve real problems. The motto is "Innovation with Honour": clear scope, open progress and real client feedback.
## Who founded TAVIX
TAVIX was founded by Logesh Visvanathan, who is the Founder and CEO. He started TAVIX to take on real problems in the automobile industry, inspired by Tesla's approach of joining hardware, software and AI.
## Motive and mission of the company
The motive of TAVIX is to turn real-world problems into working technology and to be completely open about how the work goes. We show every project, its progress, its result and the client's review in public. We also want to help learners become builders, which is why we created ONE TAVIX AI.
## What does TAVIX do
TAVIX works in five areas: Web Development and Testing; AI and ML builds; Blockchain technologies; Automotive software; Hardware plus software (IoT and embedded systems). The Portfolio section of the website lists delivered, in-progress and upcoming projects.
## Company values
Innovation with Honour. Honest scope: we do not promise what we cannot deliver. Open progress: clients can see how work is going. Real feedback: reviews are shown as given. Learning by building: we grow people by giving them real problems.
## Prices, quotes and delivery dates
TAVIX does not publish fixed prices. The TAVIX team confirms the scope, price and delivery date after reviewing the requirement. Use the contact details on the website to reach the team.
