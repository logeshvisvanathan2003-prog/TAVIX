# TAVIZ products
## What products does TAVIZ have
At the moment the available product is ONE TAVIZ AI, a gamified learning and building platform at https://one.tavixai.in. More products will be added to the Products menu of the website as they are completed.
## What is TAVIZ AI
TAVIZ AI is the assistant on the TAVIZ company website (the round button at the bottom right). It answers questions about the company, the founder, careers and hiring, the products and how to use the TAVIZ website and ONE TAVIZ AI. It does not build projects; building happens in ONE TAVIZ AI.
## What is ONE TAVIZ AI
ONE TAVIZ AI is a gamified platform for learners and builders at https://one.tavixai.in. You describe a real problem in your own words and it creates a complete multi-file project plan and code (frontend, backend, database, AI/ML), which you then learn and rebuild level by level. It runs on TAVIZ's own models, not on third-party chatbots.
## What can I do in ONE TAVIZ AI
Kingdom: a world map of quests that teach web, backend, databases, AI/ML, mobile, security and shipping, with XP, levels and badges. Endless Tower: floors of questions that get harder as you climb, with streaks, a leaderboard and insights. Build Track: a level-by-level plan for your own project, with a code editor and checks. Studio: chat with the AI to change your generated project, upload a screenshot for the vision model to read, and download the finished project as a ZIP. Tutor: ask about a lesson, your plan or a bug.
## Is ONE TAVIZ AI free
You can register and start. For pricing questions about paid or business use, the TAVIZ team confirms details.
## How do I start with ONE TAVIZ AI
Open Products in the top menu of the TAVIZ website and choose ONE TAVIZ AI, or go to https://one.tavixai.in and register. Then answer the short onboarding, enter the Kingdom, and start a New mission by describing your problem.
## Which programming languages and databases can ONE TAVIZ AI generate
Backends in Python, Node.js, Go, Java, C# and C++, and databases such as SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL and MongoDB. Generated code is meant to be learned from and reviewed before production use.
## Where did Missions, My missions and Book go
Starting a mission, tracking your missions and booking a build now live inside ONE TAVIZ AI, not on the TAVIZ company website.
