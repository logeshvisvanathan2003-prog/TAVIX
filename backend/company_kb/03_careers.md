# Careers at TAVIZ
## Does TAVIZ hire
Yes. TAVIZ hires builders who like solving real problems: developers, AI and ML engineers, embedded engineers, testers, designers and interns. See the Careers page for current roles and to apply.
## How does TAVIZ hire
The process has five steps: 1) Apply with your profile, GitHub or portfolio. 2) Portfolio review by the team. 3) A practical challenge, usually a small real mission, so we see how you work rather than how you memorise. 4) Technical conversation about your solution. 5) Conversation with the founder, then an offer and onboarding.
## What is life at TAVIZ like
Small team, real problems, direct access to the founder. You ship something real early. Progress is open: we say what is done and what is not. We learn by building, review each other's work kindly and care about doing things honestly.
## What roles are available
Full-Stack Developer (React and FastAPI), AI and ML Engineer, Embedded and IoT Engineer, QA and Test Automation Engineer, Blockchain Developer, UI/UX and Motion Designer, and Technical Content and Community Intern. Roles open and close, so check the Careers page for the current list.
## Do I need experience or a degree
Skills and proof of work matter most. A strong project, GitHub profile or practical challenge result can matter more than a certificate. Students and freshers are welcome to apply for internships.
## How do I apply
Open the Careers page from the top menu, pick a role and fill the short application form with your links. The team reads every application.
## What salary does TAVIZ offer
Compensation is discussed with shortlisted candidates and depends on role and experience. It is not published on the website.
## Is the job remote
Working arrangements are confirmed per role during the interview process.
