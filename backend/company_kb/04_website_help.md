# Using the TAVIZ websites
## How do I use the TAVIZ website
The top menu has Home, About, Products, Portfolio, Careers and TAVIZ AI. Products opens a dropdown that lists the products and links to them. The round TAVIZ AI button at the bottom right opens the assistant.
## Where can I see TAVIZ projects
In the Portfolio section of the home page: projects marked Delivered, In progress or Upcoming, with progress bars and client reviews.
## How do I leave a review
Open a delivered project in the Portfolio section and leave a rating and comment. Reviews are shown as given.
## How do I contact the founder or team
Use the contact details in the footer. For jobs use the Careers page form.
## Where is ONE TAVIZ AI
At https://one.tavixai.in. It is a separate website for learners and builders, linked from the Products menu.
## What is the difference between TAVIZ and ONE TAVIZ AI
TAVIZ (tavixai.in) is the official company website: about us, portfolio, products and careers. ONE TAVIZ AI (one.tavixai.in) is the product where you learn, play and build projects. TAVIZ AI is the assistant that answers questions on the company website.
## I have a technical problem with the website
Tell TAVIZ AI what happened and on which page. If it persists, use the contact details in the footer to reach the team.
